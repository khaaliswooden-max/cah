## Action 1: Pre-Bill Scrubbing (+$298K in 90 days)

| Benchmark | Current | Gap |
|-----------|---------|-----|
| **5.0%** denial rate | **8.7%** denial rate | 3.7% excess denials |

### How the benchmark supports this action:

- The **5.0% target** isn't arbitrary—it's the achievable industry standard for CAHs with proper revenue cycle management
- Your **$16.2M in Net Patient Revenue** × 8.7% denial rate = ~$1.41M in denied claims
- Reducing to 5.0% = ~$810K in denials → **$298K recovered**
- The 90-day timeline is realistic because pre-bill scrubbing is a process fix, not a structural change

**Operational lever:** Every 1% reduction in denial rate = ~$162K recovered

## Action 2: Optimize Staffing Ratios (+$580K in 6 months)

| Benchmark | Current | Gap |
|-----------|---------|-----|
| **52.0%** labor cost ratio | **58.2%** labor cost ratio | 6.2% excess |

### How the benchmark supports this action:

- With **$18.8M in Operating Expenses**, labor at 58.2% = ~$10.9M on staffing
- Benchmark 52% would be ~$9.8M → **$1.16M total excess**
- The action targets **$580K** (half the gap)—this is realistic because:
  - Your **bed occupancy is only 38.2%** (below 42% median)
  - Your **census shows 13 available beds** out of 25
  - Low occupancy + high labor cost = overstaffing opportunity

**Operational lever:** Your Length of Stay headroom (68.4h vs 96h limit) means you can serve the same patients with fewer shift overlaps

---

## Action 3: Expand Swing Bed Program (+$185K in 12 months)

| Benchmark | Current | Target |
|-----------|---------|--------|
| **42%** bed occupancy median | **38.2%** occupancy | **75%** swing bed utilization |

### How the benchmarks support this action:

- Current swing bed utilization: **52%**
- Target: **75%** (+23% improvement)
- You have **27.6 hours of headroom** under the 96-hour CAH limit—this is capacity you can monetize
- Swing beds bill at **SNF rates** (often higher reimbursement than acute for appropriate patients)
- The 12-month timeline reflects the need to build referral relationships and care protocols

**Operational lever:** Each swing bed day captured (instead of transferring out) improves both occupancy AND margin
