## Recommendations to Reach 90%+ Grounding

### Quick Wins (1-2 weeks)
1. **Document benchmark sources** with citations (Flex Monitoring, state data, etc.)
2. **Add confidence intervals** to ROI projections (e.g., +$298K ± 20%)
3. **Verify denial rate definition** (initial vs. final, by payer class)

### Medium-Term (1-2 months)
4. **Peer group validation** - Compare to 5-10 similar CAHs (bed size, state, case mix)
5. **Sensitivity analysis** - What if you only hit 75% of benchmark?
6. **Implementation assessment** - Current state readiness for each action

### For Full Validation
7. **Pilot one action** and measure actual vs. projected results
8. **External validation** - Have a rural health consultant review methodology
9. **Board/leadership review** - Stress-test assumptions with operational leaders
